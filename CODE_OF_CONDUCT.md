# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Health Analyzer to be a respectful, curious, and useful project for
people building personal health and fitness tools. Contributors and users should
be treated with dignity regardless of background, identity, experience level, or
health status.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and direct.
- Assume good intent, but correct unsafe behavior clearly.
- Respect privacy and health sensitivity.
- Give constructive feedback.
- Keep disagreements technical and evidence-based.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or discrimination
- Publishing private health, location, credential, or identity data
- Sexualized or violent language
- Repeated derailing of project discussions
- Encouraging unsafe medical, emergency, or medication decisions

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, restrict participation, or ban
contributors who violate this code. Serious privacy or security issues may be
handled privately first.
